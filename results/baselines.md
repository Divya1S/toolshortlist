# Stage 1 results: baselines

Generated by `python -m toolshortlist.report` from the JSON files in this directory. Do not edit by hand.

All scores are multiplied by 100. R@k is Recall@k and C@10 is Completeness@10.

ToolRet queries are searched against the whole corpus of 44,453 tools. Unseen-source tasks (28 tasks, 4,410 queries) share no source with ToolRet's training set; seen-source tasks (7 tasks, 3,551 queries) do. The split comes from [data_audit.md](data_audit.md). LiveMCPBench has 94 queries over 527 tools, and its labels follow this project's own protocol (see DATA.md).
