Produced by `python -m toolshortlist.audit`.

## Rule

A task is seen-source if its source dataset is one the training set was built from, or at least 1% of its queries are exact or near-duplicates of a training query, or at least 1% of its queries have a gold tool that matches a training positive. Every other task is unseen-source.

A query duplicates a training query if the two are identical after normalisation, or their word sets have a Jaccard overlap of at least 0.9. A gold tool matches a training positive under any of the four criteria in the last table; descriptions are near-identical at a difflib ratio of at least 0.9, and an identical description counts on its own only if it has at least 40 characters. These thresholds were fixed before the counts were seen.
